# 来信-OPC业务 Business Framework

## Thesis

OPC is not just "one person starts a company." The sellable wedge is:

```text
one operator + AI agents + repeatable workflow + human approval + quality evidence
```

来信 should sell the operating system and delivery workflow, not a generic AI tool list.

## Customer Segments

1. Solo founder / independent builder
   - Wants MVP, landing page, workflow automation, content/lead engine.
2. Small business owner
   - Has revenue and messy operations; wants AI automation without hiring a team.
3. Consultant / freelancer
   - Needs a packaged service delivery system and client-facing assets.
4. Traditional operator trying AI
   - Needs training, templates, and safe adoption with human review.

## Offers

### P0: OPC 情报周报

- Output: weekly report, opportunity ranking, tool/company signals, action list.
- Purpose: content asset, lead magnet, consulting entry.
- Validation: can it produce 3+ qualified conversations in 7 days?

### P0: AI 开发工坊

- Output: demand card, MVP, repo, test/evidence report, deployment SOP.
- Packages:
  - 1 day: idea clarification
  - 7 days: MVP prototype
  - 21 days: business system
- Validation: deliver one real internal/customer MVP with acceptance evidence.

### P0: OPC OS

- Output: lead pool, AI interview, quote, delivery tasks, status, review report.
- Validation: one operator can manage 3 parallel customer/project flows without losing state.

### P1: AgentOps 托管

- Output: scheduled research, lead scan, competitor scan, content queue, alerts.
- Validation: weekly scheduled output creates a decision or action every time.

### P1: AI 交付质检

- Output: source trail, change trail, tests, risk notes, approval, rollback plan.
- Validation: every AI-generated deliverable has a reviewable evidence card.

## Product Order

1. Web landing + intake + report view.
2. Admin workspace for leads, demand cards, tasks, reports.
3. Mini-program for light intake and status checking.
4. APP only after weekly active repeat use is proven.

## Discussion Agenda

When starting a business discussion, answer in order:

1. Who is the first buyer?
2. What painful job do they already pay for or spend time on?
3. What is the first paid offer?
4. What artifact proves value in 7 days?
5. What can be reused across customers?
6. What must stay manual for trust, quality, or relationship reasons?
7. What should the Web/mini-program/app do now, and what should wait?

## Opportunity Scoring

Use the existing 来信 information evaluation frame:

```text
可信度 25%
可行性 35%
紧迫性 25%
验证成本 15%
```

Threshold:

- >= 4.0: enter development queue.
- 3.0-3.9: validate with additional sources or interviews.
- < 3.0: background reference.

## Default Next Artifacts

Convert discussion into one of these:

- MVP spec
- workflow template
